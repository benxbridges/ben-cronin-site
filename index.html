scape.radio

2024 · Generative music

10,000 one-minute pieces of music. Always playing, never repeating.

scape.radio is a continuous player I assembled from stems I composed in Ableton — stock synthesizers, sampled drums, an 8-bit feel. Code stitches the stems into 10,000 unique one-minute pieces that accompany a collection of 10,000 digital landscapes. An experiment in letting software take over the arrangement.

    2024 · Speaking Across Time

Music is magic. It is wiggly air that enters at the ears and asks the body to move and the brain to think. I love making it, and I love helping others to make their own.

In sound, I am primarily interested with texture, contrast, arrangement, and play. It's important to me that music surprises a listener and tugs at the ear to say "hey, wake up!" — in a sea of endless music and algorithmic curation I think it's really important to be a sonic sore thumb.

In practice, I am a generalist; I am a songwriter published by Payday Records. I've released my own music on Ninja Tune, Foreign Family Collective and Universal Music. I love to write, produce, and mix, and I really love helping draw good sounds and songs out of other people.

I'd love to make a record together, or help with yours — please let me know if you'd like to connect.
